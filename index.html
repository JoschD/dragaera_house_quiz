To Which House of the Dragaeran Empire Do You Belong To?

Esteemed reader, connoisseur of both wisdom and wit! It is with the deepest sense of duty and delight that I, Paarfi of Roundwood, present to you this most erudite and illuminating examination of character- an inquiry sanctioned, reviewed, and vigorously applauded by no fewer than three Imperial academies (and frowned upon by only two).

You may, perhaps, find yourself pondering why an Easterner - accomplished in your own brief and bustling way, yet undeniably of non-Dragaeran origin - should be subjected to so refined and aristocratic a classification as that of the Great Houses. I assure you, the question is as old as the Cycle itself, and nearly as controversial. Yet, recent and entirely reputable research (the funding of which I need hardly mention was both lavish and well deserved) has uncovered the tantalizing possibility that, in exceedingly rare instances, the soul of a true Dragaeran may find itself - by some cosmic clerical error - reborn in the body of an Easterner.

It is therefore our working hypothesis, dear reader, that you may be one of these delightful anomalies. And so, through this brief but revelatory series of inquiries, we shall endeavor to discern which of the Seventeen Great Houses most closely mirrors the hidden architecture of your soul.

Answer with honesty, precision, and - should you manage it - grace. For only in truth lies the path toward the House that will guide your spirit along the Paths of the Dead. Should falsehood find its way into your answers, well… let us merely say that the Halls of Judgement have remarkably good memories, and even better record-keeping.
